How it works

  1. Pick the day (it opens on today, China time). Add photos, videos and voice memos, and type a note.
  2. Everything goes straight into the matching Dropbox day folder. You do not need the Dropbox app or Claude.
  3. Every night at 23:07 China time Claude sorts the files, transcribes voice memos, reads Amap screenshots and edits one short.
  4. Next morning, "From Claude" on that day shows what was done, post ideas, and what is waiting for you in Metricool.
  5. Nothing goes live until you approve it in Metricool.

Big videos: upload on hotel WiFi and keep the screen on. If the page will not open, try again with the VPN on.
