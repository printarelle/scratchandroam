Top 25 apps to have in China, ranked like a boarding pass

The 25 apps worth having for a trip to China, ranked from critical to optional: what each does, how to set it up, what you lose without it. Checked October 2026.

ChecklistDated and rechecked

These are the top 25 China apps for a first trip, ranked in order of importance. Each one gets a boarding pass: what it is for, how to set it up, why it ranks where it does, and what happens if you land without it. The first five are critical, the last five are optional, and two of those come with a warning.

It is the written version of my Scratch & Roam Short, in the same order. The pass rows are my words from the video; the fine print on each pass was checked on 18 October 2026 against the sources at the bottom. Where I could not confirm something, the pass says so. For the full setup of the first dozen apps, use my China travel apps guide.

Quick answers

What are the most important apps to have in China?

Alipay and WeChat for paying and messaging, a travel eSIM or VPN for Google and WhatsApp, and Amap for maps. These are the first four to five passes on this list, and all of them need to be set up at home.

Which China apps do I need to set up before I fly?

Alipay, WeChat, an eSIM or VPN, 12306, Trip.com and your translator packs. They need a passport check, a text code, or a download from a store that does not work in China, so do them at home.

Which apps on the list can I skip?

Waygo (not updated since 2017) and Kuli Kuli (a paywall) are the two with warnings. DeepL is optional and I could not confirm it works without a VPN in China. Ele.me is now called Taobao Flash Sale.

Do I need a VPN in China if I have an eSIM?

Usually not on mobile data, because travel eSIM plans for China say they route data outside China. A VPN still helps on hotel Wi-Fi, and it has to be installed and tested before you fly.

Which translator app is best for China?

Download the Chinese pack in Google Translate or Microsoft Translator at home, add Baidu Translate for signs and menus, and keep Pleco for single characters. Pick two and test them before you fly.
