China arrival card in Alipay on your phone, step by step

Filling in the China arrival card online with Alipay on your phone? Here is the whole form step by step. Upload your passport and most of the details fill in for you.

If you already have Alipay set up for paying in China, the arrival card is inside it too. The passport page fills in most of the form, you check it, and you save the QR code at the end. Prefer a computer? Use the PC guide.

Step by step

  1. 1

    What the arrival card is

    China's arrival card is filled in online before you land. The official version also lives inside Alipay as a mini program, and it is free.

  2. 2

    Get ready

    Have your passport, your flight number, the address of where you are staying and your reason for travel next to you. Install Alipay first if you have not yet.

  3. 3

    Find the mini program in Alipay

    Open Alipay and search for the arrival card mini program. Open it and agree to the terms.

  4. 4

    Agree and passport page

    Take a photo of the photo page of your passport. The app reads it and fills in your name, passport number, nationality and dates. Check each one.

  5. 5

    Basic information

    Confirm the basic details the passport page supplied.

  6. 6

    Personal information

    Add your contact details and anything the passport could not supply.

  7. 7

    Travel information

    Flight number, where you will stay, how long, and why you are travelling. Use the address exactly as your booking shows it.

  8. 8

    Sign and confirm

    Sign on the screen and confirm. Read the summary once more before you submit.

  9. 9

    Save your QR code

    Save the QR code, download the PDF and send it to your email, so it is on your phone even without signal.

Questions people ask

Do I have to show the QR code at the border?

In our own experience nobody asked us to show it, but it is better to have it. Save it as a PDF and send it to your email so it is on your phone either way.

Does the arrival card cost anything?

No. The official card on the National Immigration Administration site is free. If a site asks you to pay, it is not the official one.

Can I do it on my phone instead of a computer?

Yes. The same card is inside Alipay as a mini program. There is a separate guide and video for that version.

What do I need to have ready?

Your passport, your flight number, where you will stay in China and the reason for the trip. The passport page fills most of the form for you.

Rules change. Everything here was checked on 2026-10-15 against the sources below. Before you fly, open the official page once more.
