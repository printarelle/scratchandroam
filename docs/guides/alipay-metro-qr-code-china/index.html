Alipay metro QR code in China: how to add it, step by step

How to add the Alipay metro and bus QR code for China: pick the city, claim the free card, enter your payment password, and fix the wrong-city code.

The Alipay metro QR code is what you hold over the gate to ride the metro or bus in a Chinese city. I set mine up at home in Ireland, for Chengdu, before our December trip. This guide shows how I did it on my own phone: where Transport is, how to pick the city, how to claim the free card, and what to do when the QR code is for the wrong city.

The screenshots are frames from my own screen recording. The facts about how it is charged come from the official Alipay+ documentation and are marked official. Things I could not confirm are marked as such. Checked on 18 October 2026.

Quick answers

How do I add the metro QR code in Alipay?

Open Alipay, tap Transport, pick your city, tap Bus & Metro, tap Agree and obtain card, then enter your Alipay payment password. When you see Card collected successfully, tap Use now to open the QR code.

Do I need a different QR code for each city?

Yes. One third-party guide says each city needs its own ride code and that activating Beijing's does not cover Shanghai. Pick the city, claim its card, and repeat when you move on.

Is the Alipay metro card free, and how do I pay?

The card screen I used said Free card. The official Alipay+ documentation describes a ride first, pay later model: the fare is deducted after you exit a station or leave a vehicle, from the payment method linked to your wallet. Fares themselves differ by city, so I give no price.

Why does the gate not open with my QR code?

The usual causes are the wrong city selected, or using the shop payment code instead of the Transport code. Switch the city at the top left of the Alipay home screen. If it still fails, buy a single-journey ticket at the machine. The Alipay+ documentation says the ticket machines in every mainland metro station accept Alipay.

Can I set it up before I fly?

It worked on my phone in Ireland for a city I have not reached yet. One third-party guide says some users abroad see an empty Transport tab and may need to activate on arrival, so test it at home and have a plan for when you land.

The 8 steps, in order

  1. 1

    Set up Alipay at home first

    The metro QR code lives inside your Alipay account, so the account has to work first: identity check, a linked bank card and your payment password. The steps are in my China travel apps guide. One third-party guide says the account needs a linked foreign card and identity verification before the transit feature works. I did not find an official Alipay page that lists the requirements for visitors, so follow what your own app asks for.

    Frames from my own screen recording of Alipay. Tap a picture to open it larger.

  2. 2

    Open Alipay and tap Transport

    On the Alipay home screen, Transport is in the top row, next to Scan, Pay/Receive and Pocket. This is not the same thing as the payment code you show in a shop. A third-party guide warns that the shop payment QR does not open a metro gate, so it has to be the Transport code.

    Frames from my own screen recording of Alipay. Tap a picture to open it larger.

  3. 3

    Pick the city you are going to

    My Alipay was set to Cork. I am going to Chengdu in a month and a half, so I opened the city list and tapped Chengdu. Each city has its own code: another guide says activating Beijing's does not cover Shanghai. Pick the city you are about to ride in, and repeat for each city you visit.

    Frames from my own screen recording of Alipay. Tap a picture to open it larger.

  4. 4

    Tap Bus & Metro

    Back on the Transport tab, the Metro card was not showing yet, only Taxi and a map. After the city was set, the BusMetro icon appeared in the top row. Tap it. Parts of the page are in Chinese, so the floating Translate button is useful. If the translation seems stuck, be patient, it can take a moment.

    Frames from my own screen recording of Alipay. Tap a picture to open it larger.

  5. 5

    Tap Agree and obtain card

    The next screen is called Obtain card to take Metro, and it names the metro provider for that city. The screen says Free card. The agreement is in Chinese. Read it with Translate before you tap the blue Agree and obtain card button. The official Alipay+ documentation says the fare is taken after your ride, from the payment method you have linked, on a ride first, pay later basis.

    Frames from my own screen recording of Alipay. Tap a picture to open it larger.

  6. 6

    Enter your Alipay payment password

    Alipay asks for the payment password you set up for paying. This is the password screen that goes black when you record the screen, which is why my video shows a notice instead. Type it on your own phone, in private.

    Frames from my own screen recording of Alipay. Tap a picture to open it larger.

  7. 7

    Card collected: tap Use now to open the QR code

    When it works you get Card collected successfully and a Use now button, and Use now takes you to your QR code. The same Bus & Metro card idea applies to buses and to any city you add; I also added another city the same way, and there is an option on the screen to activate all available transports. At the gate, hold the code over the reader. Two third-party guides say to turn the screen brightness up and hold it steady for a second or two. At a bus reader, scan before you go in.

  8. 8

    If the QR code is for the wrong city, switch the city

    If the code does not match the city you are standing in, the gate will not open. Tap the city name at the top left of the Alipay home screen and choose the right one. A guide updated on 14 October 2026 calls a wrong city selection a common reason codes fail. If a gate still refuses you, step aside and buy a single-journey ticket at the machine, and keep a little cash for buses.
